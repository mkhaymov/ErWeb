Elena Roginsky Arts

About

I am using different media for my artwork - oil on canvas, watercolour, pastel and original print. Currently my favorite theme is cityscapes. I life-paint them on the streets of Toronto and elsewhere. My work is held in collections in Canada, U.S.A., and the U.K.

Elena Roginsky captures a moment in time through a process of observation and introspection. Her works are emotional and painterly, and rooted in the historical tradition of painting.
